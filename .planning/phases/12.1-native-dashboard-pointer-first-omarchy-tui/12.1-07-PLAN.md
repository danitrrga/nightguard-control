    watcher that fires on the theme files can read a stale name — and keep showing it,
    because the watcher already consumed the event. Re-reading is what makes that
    self-healing.

    the right, row 6 blank.** The six section labels are the **only** place tracking by

    `letter_spacing` property. Nowhere else.

    **Rows 7-10, the hero region.** `DayRamp` on 7, the permission channel on 8, the hour
    axis on 9, and the **reserved read-out row** on 10 — reserved so nothing reflows when it
    fills. Row 11 blank.

    verdict and curfew; `T H I S  W E E K` on the right with the token meter, resets, may-weaken
    and grace. The countdown is `Digits`, **thin set** — `text-style: bold` switches Textual
    to the heavier `DIGITS3X3_BOLD`, which reads more like terminal art than the desktop it
    is imitating. `Digits` is allowed exactly **once** per screen; a second `display`-mass
    element breaks the never-stack-two-hero-materials rule. Its character set includes `:`,
    so `HH:MM:SS` is safe. The countdown's colour follows the verdict role; the glyphs carry
    no accent. Row 18 blank.

    **Keylines are `Rule`, and `Rule` must be overridden** — plan 04 wrote
    `Rule { margin: 0; color: $foreground 12%; }`. Unstyled it costs 3 rows instead of 1 and
    paints in `$secondary`, which equals `$accent` on the omarchy `Theme`.

    **Remove `yield Header()`** from `compose` and drop `Header` from the import. Leave
    `Footer` for Task 2 so each task's diff stays reviewable.

    **What survives unchanged:** `verdict_display` and its map, including unknown→LOCKED

    still renders a full-screen message rather than a broken hero; `refresh_countdown()`'s
    name and signature, called from `app.py` on both screens; and `toggle_ledger()`.

    Verdict copy is exactly the contract's table — glyph, word, role, caption and the
    uppercase meta line travel together, always. The `curfew until 05:30` style captions and
    the `CURFEW · 7H 38M REMAINING` meta lines are the contract's strings, not paraphrases.
