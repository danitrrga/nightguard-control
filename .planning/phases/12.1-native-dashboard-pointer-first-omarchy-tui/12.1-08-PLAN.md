<objective>
Make the two guardrails checkable rather than claimed, and finish removing the framework
chrome.

The layout cannot quietly outgrow the sanctioned window: the windowrule pins `size 875 600`,
which at the live terminal font is exactly 135 × 46 cells, and the overflow assertion was
proved able to discriminate before planning — it passes at 40 and 46 rows of content and
fails at 60. The accent reserve is likewise a **counted** assertion against the 6210-cell
screen, not a stated ratio: measured, the composed screen spends 154 cells (2.48 %) against
a 310-cell ceiling, and painting the frame in the accent takes it to 424 (6.83 %).

Purpose: a guardrail nobody counts is a preference. These two are the difference between the
aesthetic surviving the next six months and drifting.
Output: controls 9 and 10, the degradation handler, and an editor with no chrome.
</objective>
